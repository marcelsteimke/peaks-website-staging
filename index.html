Blatt PEAKS · 1 : 25 000Zürich

Some peaks are higher than others. Much higher.

Impact works the same way: a few ways of doing good rise far above everything else. There are many such summits, and no single right one to climb. PEAKS is an office for people who search for the highest peaks they can reach, and then set out to climb them. People who stay open to changing paths as their map and the terrain shift, and who would rather climb together than alone.

Find Us in Zurich

05 · Community

Our Community

PEAKS hosts a diverse and talented community of researchers, professionals, and organizations working at the intersection of Effective Altruism, AI safety and biosecurity. Our members are united by a shared commitment to using evidence and reason to tackle the world's most pressing problems.

From established organizations to independent researchers, our community spans multiple disciplines and approaches, creating a rich environment for collaboration and knowledge exchange.
